# Scene 22 — The Instantaneous Center of Rotation

Because the bottom point has zero instantaneous velocity, the wheel's velocity field can be described as if the entire disk were instantaneously rotating about that point. But do not mistake this for a physical hinge. The contact point is not permanently attached to the ground; a different material point reaches the bottom a moment later, and the point currently at the bottom generally has nonzero acceleration even though its instantaneous velocity is zero.
