# Scene 09 — Pull a Disk Through Its Center

Now apply a horizontal force through the center of the disk. First imagine there were no friction. The force passes through the center, so by itself it produces no torque about the center. The disk would translate toward the right without acquiring enough clockwise rotation, so the bottom surface would tend to slip forward relative to the ground. Static friction must therefore point backward, not because the disk moves forward, but because the contact would otherwise slip forward.
