# Scene 14 — Solving the Forward-Friction Case

Solving translation, rotation, and the rolling constraint together for this case gives friction equal to F over three, toward the right, and acceleration equal to four F over three m. This is why the statement 'friction opposes motion' can be misleading: the center of mass moves right, the applied force points right, and static friction also points right. What friction opposes is the relative slipping tendency at the contact.
