# Scene 28 (Bonus) — Friction Converts Spin Into Rolling

Here is the same idea from a different angle, simulated rather than scripted. A disk starts spinning in place on rough ground with no initial forward velocity at all. Friction between the disk and the ground does the rest: it slows the spin and speeds up the center of mass until the rolling condition, v center of mass equals omega R, is satisfied. Nothing here was scripted frame by frame; a physics engine computed every position from the friction coefficient alone.
