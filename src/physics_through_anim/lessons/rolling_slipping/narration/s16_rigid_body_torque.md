# Scene 16 — Build a Rigid Body From Particles

To understand rolling more deeply, we need to understand why torque produces angular acceleration. Imagine constructing a rigid body out of many small particles. For a particle at distance r from the axis, an angular acceleration alpha corresponds to a tangential force, and that force produces a torque proportional to mass times r squared. Summing over every particle defines the moment of inertia, and the sum of all torques becomes torque equals I times alpha.
