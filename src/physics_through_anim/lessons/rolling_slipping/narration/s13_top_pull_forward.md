# Scene 13 — Can Friction Point Forward?

Now change just one thing: instead of pulling through the center, pull horizontally at the top. The center still wants to accelerate right, but now the applied force also produces a strong clockwise torque, and rotation develops faster than translation. So the bottom of the disk tends to slip backward, and friction opposes that relative slipping. Therefore static friction points forward, even though the entire disk is moving forward.
