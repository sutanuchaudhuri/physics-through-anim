# Scene 04 — Rock rolling (skin over a rolling disk)

TODO narration (draft): This boulder is just a picture — a rock skin — wrapped
around a rolling disk. The physics never looks at the jagged shape: it sees a
disk with a radius, a centre of mass, a contact point on the ground, and weight
pulling down at the centre. As it rolls, the turn angle is minus the distance
over the radius, with no drift. Swap the rock for a wheel, a barrel, or a photo
and the physics is identical. Frozen at the end, the rolling velocity field shows
each rim point moving perpendicular to the line from the contact point — fastest
at the top, zero at the ground.
