# Scene 16 — Series & parallel springs

TODO narration (draft): Springs combine in two basic ways. Put them end to end —
in series — and the same force stretches every coil, so the group is softer than
any one spring: the inverse stiffnesses add. Put them side by side — in parallel,
sharing the same two attachment points — and they share the load, so the group is
stiffer: the stiffnesses add directly. Each bank here is built with a single
helper call, and the effective stiffness is just a label computed from the parts.
