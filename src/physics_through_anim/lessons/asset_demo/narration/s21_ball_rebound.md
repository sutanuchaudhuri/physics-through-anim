# Scene 21 — Rebound on an incline

TODO narration (draft): A ball flies in and strikes a ramp. The ramp gives us one
thing: the direction pointing straight out of its surface — the normal. Split the
incoming velocity into a part along the surface and a part into it. The bounce
flips the into-the-surface part and shrinks it by the restitution — how bouncy the
contact is — while the along-the-surface part carries straight through. That is
the whole rule: reflect the velocity about the normal, scale the normal part by e.
The ball leaves along the new direction and flies off in a fresh parabola. The
speeds jump at the instant of contact, but the ball never teleports.
