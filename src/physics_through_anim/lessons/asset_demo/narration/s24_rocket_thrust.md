# Scene 24 — Rocket thrust

TODO narration (draft): A rocket carries its own propellant and throws it out the
back. Every second it ejects a certain mass of exhaust at high speed downward. By
conservation of momentum, throwing mass one way pushes the rocket the other way:
the thrust equals the exhaust speed times the rate at which mass leaves. It is the
same momentum-flux idea as sand on a belt, just in reverse — emission instead of
collection. Look at the nozzle N: that is where the exhaust leaves and the thrust
is born.
