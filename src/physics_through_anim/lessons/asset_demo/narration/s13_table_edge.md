# Scene 13 — Cylinder at a table edge

TODO narration (draft): A cylinder rolls along a table top. While it is on the
flat top, the surface pushes straight up at the contact point beneath its centre.
When it reaches the corner, the contact suddenly changes: it is no longer a patch
on a flat surface but a single point at the sharp edge, and the body starts to
pivot about it. The moment the normal force drops to zero, the cylinder loses
contact entirely and leaves the table as a spinning projectile. The shape of the
surface tells us the direction of every contact force; the trajectory tells us
when each change happens.
