# Scene 05 — Cylinder on an incline (seated by the constraint)

TODO narration (draft): We just say "place it on the ramp" — no coordinates. The
framework seats the cylinder tangent to the slope, because a round body on a wall
is the same non-penetration constraint we already have: the centre sits exactly
one radius out along the surface normal, touching but never piercing. As it rolls
down, the centre travels along the slope at that fixed offset, so it stays in
contact the whole way — being on the surface is part of the motion, not something
we place by hand. The free-body diagram shows weight down at the centre, the
normal out of the slope, and friction up the slope at the contact point.
