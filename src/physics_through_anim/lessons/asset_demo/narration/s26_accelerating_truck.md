# Scene 26 — Two frames, one block

TODO narration (draft): A block sits in a truck that suddenly accelerates. To
someone standing on the ground, the block accelerates along with the truck, pushed
by friction — and only real forces act: the normal force, gravity, and friction.
But to someone riding inside the truck, the block just sits there, at rest. To
explain why it stays put in that frame, the rider has to invent a force pushing the
block backwards — the pseudo-force, minus mass times the truck's acceleration. It
is not a real force; nothing is pushing the block. We draw it dashed and grey so
you never confuse it with the real ones. It is simply the price of using an
accelerating frame.
