TODO narration (draft): A string has a natural length. When its two ends are
closer than that length, the extra rope has nowhere to go, so it hangs slack and
sags. As we pull one end away, the slack is taken up, and the moment the ends are
as far apart as the string is long, it snaps straight and taut. The picture
follows the ends — the drawn length is never fixed.
