# Scene 03 — Supports & contact (walls cannot be pierced)

TODO narration (draft): Every support is a wall at some angle, and a wall is
never drawn with a free-body diagram — it only pushes back on the body touching
it. On a running conveyor the block slides and the contact material keeps
changing; when the belt stops, the same block simply rests. Walls and floors are
impenetrable: when a cylinder rolls into the corner where the floor meets a ramp,
it seats against both surfaces at once and stops — it can never pierce a wall.
The framework clamps the geometry so no rolling or trajectory update crosses a
boundary.
