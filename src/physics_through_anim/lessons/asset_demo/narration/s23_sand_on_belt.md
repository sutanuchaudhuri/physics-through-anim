# Scene 23 — Sand on a moving belt

TODO narration (draft): Sand pours steadily onto a conveyor belt that is moving to
the right. Each grain lands going straight down, with no sideways speed — but the
belt is moving, so the belt has to grab each grain and speed it up to belt speed.
That takes a forward force, and by momentum flux it equals the rate of mass
arriving times the belt speed. Look at point P, where the sand meets the belt:
that is where the belt keeps having to push. And a marked grain, Q, once it lands,
simply rides along with the belt — a point of interest that moves with the body.
