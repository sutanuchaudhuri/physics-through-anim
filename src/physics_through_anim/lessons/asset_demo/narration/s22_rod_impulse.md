# Scene 22 — An off-centre impulse

TODO narration (draft): Hit a body squarely through its centre of mass and it just
translates. Hit it off-centre — at the end of a rod, the way a bat meets a ball or
a rod strikes a wall — and something richer happens: the centre of mass still gains
velocity equal to the impulse over the mass, but the body also starts to spin,
because the impulse has a lever arm about the centre. That spin is the cross
product of the offset with the impulse, divided by the moment of inertia. The end
that was struck leads the motion while the centre of mass sails straight ahead.
There is even a special spot — the centre of percussion — where the blow causes no
jolt at the pivot. That is the sweet spot of a bat.
