# Scene 15 — Mass on a spring

TODO narration (draft): A block sits on a smooth floor, tied to the wall by a
spring. Pull it aside and let go, and it oscillates back and forth in simple
harmonic motion. Watch the coil itself: it visibly stretches when the block moves
away from the wall and compresses when it swings back, and the spring force always
points toward the natural length — it reverses direction exactly as the block
passes through equilibrium. Beside the motion, a phase portrait plots velocity
against displacement. Because energy is conserved, that curve is a closed
ellipse, and its cursor keeps perfect time with the oscillation.
