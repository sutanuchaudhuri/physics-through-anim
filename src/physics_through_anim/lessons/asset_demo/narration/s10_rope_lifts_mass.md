# Scene 10 — Rope over a pulley lifts a mass (no slip)

TODO narration (draft): A rope does not touch a pulley at a single point — it
wraps it. It meets the wheel at two contact points, rides an arc over the rim
between them, and leaves as two straight ends: one pulled by a hand at about a
hundred and twenty degrees, the other holding a mass. As the hand pulls the rope
out, the wheel turns without slipping — the length of rim that rotates past equals
the rope paid out — and the mass rises by exactly that much.
